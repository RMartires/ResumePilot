# Traffic growth before monetization — action checklist

Short operating list derived from the full research report in this folder.

## Do now (this week)

1. **Keep the core free** — ATS checker and resume builder stay usable without a paywall.
2. **Finish Search Console** — sitemap submitted; request indexing for `/`, `/templates`, `/tools/ats-checker`, `/tools/resume-score`, `/about`.
3. **Define activation** — e.g. user gets an ATS result and applies a fix, or completes and exports a resume. Track that, not just pageviews.
4. **Add funnel analytics** — checker started/completed, editor opened, export, return visit. Do not send resume text to analytics.
5. **Improve checker → editor** — fast result, clear next step, signup only when saving or exporting.

## Next 30 days

6. **Publish 5 strong SEO clusters** — role examples, ATS templates, skills/objectives, checker landing. Each page must be genuinely different.
7. **Internal linking** — connect templates, examples, checker, and feature pages.
8. **One founder post per week** — teardown, ATS tip, or one bullet improved. Repurpose on LinkedIn; optional Short or YouTube.
9. **Help in 2–3 communities** — answer questions first; mention ResumePilot only when relevant and allowed.
10. **Interview 10 users** — five who finished the checker, five who dropped off.

## Days 31–60

11. **Partner outreach** — ~10 targets per week: small career centers, bootcamps, coaches. Offer a free workshop or co-branded page.
12. **Product Hunt** — only when onboarding and activation are solid; stay active in comments launch day.
13. **Review Search Console weekly** — impressions, clicks, queries; expand what drives checker and editor use.

## Days 61–90

14. **One small original report** — opt-in aggregate ATS findings (privacy-safe methodology).
15. **Expand SEO selectively** — add clusters only where pages index and activate users.
16. **Test paid interest** — waitlist or survey for tailoring, AI, tracker; no full pricing launch yet.

## Avoid for now

- Mass-generated near-duplicate role pages
- Spammy self-promotion on Reddit or LinkedIn
- Paid backlink schemes
- Chrome extension or heavy integrations before repeat usage is proven
- Broad paid plan before activation and repeat use are stable

## When to add a paid plan

Require all of the following (adjust numbers to your volume):

- 8+ weeks of reliable funnel data
- Hundreds of **activated** users per month (not visits alone)
- Users returning to tailor, export, or track applications again
- ~30+ explicit paid-intent signals (interviews, waitlist, surveys)
- A natural limit users hit repeatedly (scans, AI, exports, collaboration)
